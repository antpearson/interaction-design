01 · Reveal on scroll

The most common move in scrollytelling: something appears when it reaches a line on the screen. Four variants: one element, a loop over many, a staggered list that stays once shown, and a scrubbed reveal where the movement is tied to the scrollbar instead of played once.

Change SHOW_MARKERS to true in the script to see the start and end lines ScrollTrigger is using.

One element

Each one on its own

Three boxes share a class. A loop gives each its own ScrollTrigger, so each fires when it arrives, not when the first one does.

Second box

If you put scrollTrigger on one tween targeting all three, they would all animate together the moment the first came into view. That is almost never what you want.
