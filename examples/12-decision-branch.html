12 · Scroll stops until the reader decides

A branching beat. The page literally ends at the stage: the sections below it are display: none, so there is nothing to scroll to. Two buttons appear. Each plays a different animation (one turns the hidden tiles over and lifts the matching ones with a glow; the other slides yours away and reveals what you missed), then the matching conclusion is switched on and ScrollTrigger.refresh() tells GSAP the page just got longer. The reader scrolls on.

This is simpler and more reliable than fighting the browser with overflow: hidden to "lock" scrolling, and it works on phones. The quiz, the vote, the "which would you choose" are all this pattern.
