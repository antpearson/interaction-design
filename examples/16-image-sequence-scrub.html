16 · Scrub through a sequence of rendered frames

The Apple product-page trick, and the practical way to get 3D on a scroller without writing 3D code. Render your model as a sequence of still images (Blender: a turntable, something assembling, a camera move; 40 to 120 frames), then draw whichever frame matches the scroll position onto a <canvas>. GSAP tweens a frame number; ScrollTrigger scrubs it.

The frames here are placeholders drawn by a script (a stack of blocks growing and turning). Replace frames/frame-001.webp … with yours and change FRAME_COUNT. Keep each frame small: 1200px wide and WebP at quality 75 is plenty.
