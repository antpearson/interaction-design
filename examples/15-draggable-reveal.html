15 · Drag something along a track to reveal the story

Not every interaction has to be a scroll. Draggable lets the reader pull the handle along the track; its position (0 to 1 across the track) drives a paused timeline, so notes appear as it passes them and disappear if it reverses. Click or tap the handle and it does a flip. Works with mouse, touch and pen. The handle is a block here; make it a vehicle, a figure, a slider knob.
