03 · A marker that rolls along the bottom as you scroll

An indicator tied to how far through the whole page the reader is. The trigger is the document itself, from top to bottom, and the marker's position, rotation and the number on it all come from one progress value. Swap the shape for a ball, a wheel, a figure, a vehicle: anything that should travel the width of the screen as the reader travels the length of the page.
