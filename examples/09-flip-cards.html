09 · Tiles that flip on hover, tap and scroll

Three ways to turn something over: a tile, a card, a photo with a caption on the back. Hover (desktop) flips while the pointer is on it. Tap or click toggles it and stays, which is what touch screens need. Scroll flips a whole row one after another when it comes into view. All of it is one property: rotationY. The CSS does the 3D, GSAP does the turning. A question with the answer on the back, a before and after, a "who is this" reveal.
