GSAP and ScrollTrigger examples

Nineteen small, self-contained pages. Each one does one thing and is commented line by line. Open the page, scroll it, then open the file and read the script at the bottom. Copy what you need into your own js/main.js.

Every page loads GSAP from a CDN, so you need to be online. Nothing to install.

The examples

  1. 01 · Reveal on scroll

    Fade and rise when an element reaches a line. One element, a loop, a staggered list that stays, and a scrubbed version tied to the scrollbar.

    toggleActions · once · stagger · scrub · utils.toArray

  2. 02 · Pin a scene and scrub a timeline

    The engine behind scrollytelling: a screen-sized stage held still while a timeline of beats is scrubbed through it.

    pin · scrub · end: "+=300%" · labels · onUpdate

  3. 03 · A marker rolling along the bottom

    An indicator tied to how far through the whole page you are: position, rotation and a number from one progress value.

    trigger: document · progress · invalidateOnRefresh

  4. 04 · Sticky picture, scrolling steps

    Text blocks scroll past a fixed picture and each one changes it when it arrives. The BBC and Pudding pattern.

    ScrollTrigger.create · onEnter · onEnterBack

  5. 05 · Pinned title, depth counter, darkening background

    Deep Sea style: a title that stays, a counter that reads out scroll depth, a background that darkens. Plus a number that counts up once.

    pinSpacing: false · endTrigger · tween a plain object · snap

  6. 06 · A marker following a drawn path

    A route draws itself in while a marker travels along it and labels appear as it passes.

    MotionPathPlugin · DrawSVGPlugin · SVG

  7. 07 · A section that scrolls sideways

    Scroll down, the panels move left. Includes triggering things inside the moving panels, and a stacked fallback for phones.

    pin · containerAnimation · snap · matchMedia breakpoints

  8. 08 · Deal out, turn over, take back

    A pinned scene and a long labelled timeline with many moving parts: tiles dealt from a pile to four slots, flipped, taken back; tokens and pop-up labels; a centre row that fills one tile at a time.

    function-based from() values · labels · nested timelines · 3D flip

  9. 09 · Tiles that flip on hover, tap and scroll

    One property, rotationY, three triggers. Keyboard and touch friendly.

    paused tween · play/reverse · hover media query

  10. 10 · Typewriter text and split-text titles

    Type a sentence letter by letter; make a title's letters rise in one at a time and glow; fade a paragraph in word by word.

    TextPlugin · SplitText · yoyo repeat

  11. 11 · Hotspots on a picture that open pop-ups

    Plus buttons over a drawing, each opening an animated panel with content from a data object. Escape, backdrop and focus handled.

    paused timeline · autoAlpha · repeat pulse · accessibility

  12. 12 · Scroll stops until the reader decides

    Two buttons, two different outcome animations, and the rest of the page only exists once a choice is made.

    branching · ScrollTrigger.refresh() · eventCallback

  13. 13 · Pick from columns, build a result

    Choose one from each of three columns and a result card swaps in with a drawing and a sentence. A side panel slides in for more.

    data-driven · .call() · drawer

  14. 14 · Glossary that expands, terms that explain themselves

    Click a word in the text for a tooltip; click a glossary entry and its definition slides open.

    height: "auto" · positioning from getBoundingClientRect

  15. 15 · Drag something along a track

    The reader drags a handle; its position drives a paused timeline. Click it for a flip.

    Draggable · timeline.progress() · keyboard fallback

  16. 16 · Scrub through rendered frames

    Blender renders, or any sequence of stills, drawn to a canvas as you scroll. The practical route to 3D.

    canvas · preload · snap: "frame"

  17. 17 · Transitions between scenes

    Crossfade, background colour per section, a sparkle burst, and a fade to black.

    overwrite · onEnter/onLeaveBack · generated elements

  18. 18 · A cover with a picture slider

    Dots and arrows crossfade photos with a slow zoom. Optional autoplay.

    utils.wrap · delayedCall · not scroll-driven

  19. 19 · Scrub a video with the scrollbar

    Tween currentTime. With a note on how the file must be encoded for it to be smooth.

    video · loadedmetadata

Adding GSAP to your own project

Put these just before your own script, at the bottom of index.html. Only load the plugins you use. The version number is pinned so your page does not change under you.

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.15.1/dist/gsap.min.js"></script>
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.15.1/dist/ScrollTrigger.min.js"></script>
<!-- optional plugins, same pattern: MotionPathPlugin, DrawSVGPlugin, TextPlugin,
     SplitText, Draggable, Flip, ScrollToPlugin … all free since GSAP 3.13 -->
<script src="js/main.js"></script>

Then, at the top of js/main.js:

gsap.registerPlugin(ScrollTrigger);   // and any other plugin you loaded

Things that go wrong, in order of likelihood

Nothing happens, console says "Invalid property scrollTrigger"You forgot gsap.registerPlugin(ScrollTrigger), or the plugin script is missing or loaded after your script.
Things fire at the wrong scroll positionTurn on markers: true and look. Usually an image above the trigger loaded late and pushed everything down. Give images width and height attributes, or call ScrollTrigger.refresh() on window.load.
A pinned element jumps or disappearsPinning uses position: fixed. It breaks inside any parent with transform, filter, perspective or overflow: hidden. Move the pinned element out, or pin the parent instead.
Everything animates at onceYou put one scrollTrigger on a tween that targets many elements. Loop with gsap.utils.toArray() and make one per element (example 01).
It works on desktop, breaks on a phoneHover does not exist on touch. Address bars resize the viewport. Use gsap.matchMedia() to give phones a simpler version (example 07) and test in the real device, not just the dev-tools emulator.
The page stuttersAnimate x, y, scale, rotation and opacity: the browser can do those on the GPU. Avoid width, height, top, left, margin and big box-shadow changes on many elements. Resize your images.
Reduced motionSome readers have asked their system for less motion. Wrap your animations in gsap.matchMedia().add("(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)", …) as every example here does, and make sure the page still makes sense without them.
