05 · Pinned title, depth counter, darkening background

Three things from neal.fun's Deep Sea, built the GSAP way. The section title stays pinned at the top for a while and then scrolls off. A counter at the bottom reads out how deep you are, driven by scroll progress. The background colour darkens the further down you go.

At the end: a second counter that counts up once when it enters the screen (a sum of money, a population, a score, a year).
