04 · Sticky picture, scrolling steps

The "step" pattern used by the BBC, the Pudding and most newsroom scrollytelling. A picture holds still while short blocks of text scroll past it, and each block changes the picture when it reaches the middle of the screen. One ScrollTrigger.create() per step, no scrubbing, so the reader can stop and read.

Here the "picture" is an SVG with four layers that build up a house. Yours might be four photographs stacked on top of each other, a map with a route that extends, a diagram that gains parts, or a scene where things light up.
