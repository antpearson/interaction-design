18 · A cover with a picture slider

Not scroll-driven: a cover where the reader presses dots or arrows to change the photo. Each change is a GSAP crossfade with a slow zoom on the incoming picture. Arrow keys work too. Autoplay is there if you want it (AUTOPLAY_SECONDS); it pauses while the pointer is over the cover.
