07 · A section that scrolls sideways

The reader keeps scrolling down, but the section moves left. Useful for a sequence of options, a filmstrip of photos, a menu that "slides to the left" to reveal more. The track is pinned and translated by exactly its overflow, so the distance is always right.

The second half shows containerAnimation: how to trigger something inside a horizontal panel when that panel arrives, which normal ScrollTriggers cannot measure.
