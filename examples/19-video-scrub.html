19 · Scrub a video with the scrollbar

The video does not play; its currentTime is tweened, so scrolling moves through it forwards and backwards. This only feels smooth if the file is encoded for it: every frame a keyframe, no audio, short. Re-encode yours with ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -an -g 1 -crf 23 -movflags +faststart out.mp4. Without that, the browser has to decode from the last keyframe on every scroll step and it stutters. If you cannot re-encode, use the image-sequence approach in example 16 instead.
