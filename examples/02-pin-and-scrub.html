02 · Pin a scene and scrub a timeline

The engine behind most scrollytelling. One screen-sized "stage" is pinned, and a GSAP timeline of several beats is scrubbed by the scrollbar across a set distance. The long sequence in example 08 and the decision in example 12 are both built on this.

Look for pin: true, end: "+=300%" and scrub: 1, and the way beats are placed on the timeline with labels.
