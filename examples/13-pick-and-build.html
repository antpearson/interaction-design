13 · Pick from columns, build a result

A "make your own" interactive: three columns of choices, a fourth that shows what you made. Picking an option pops it, and the result card swaps out and back in with the new drawing and a sentence describing it. A "More" button slides a bigger panel in from the right. The options are a plain object at the top of the script: edit that, not the HTML. Swap the shape for a character, a recipe, an outfit, a route.
