08 · Deal out, turn over, take back: a long labelled timeline

A pinned scene with many moving parts and many beats. Tiles are dealt from a pile to four slots in turn, two of them flip to show their faces, tokens appear, one slot "passes" (its tiles go back to the pile and it dims), short labels pop up and vanish, and tiles slide into a centre row one at a time. Any game, any "things handed out and taken back" story, is a re-skin of this.

Two things to study. The deal uses gsap.from() with function-based start positions measured from the pile, so it survives a resize. Every beat is added at a label, so the timeline reads like a script and you can move whole beats by changing one line.
