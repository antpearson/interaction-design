10 · Typewriter text and split-text titles

Two plugins for text. TextPlugin types a string into an element letter by letter. SplitText breaks existing text into characters, words or lines so each can be animated on its own (both are free since GSAP 3.13). Each is started by a ScrollTrigger when it enters the screen.
