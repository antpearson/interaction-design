11 · Hotspots on a picture that open pop-ups

A static, interactive scene: a drawing or photo with "+" buttons over its parts. Click one and a panel animates in with a title, a picture and some text pulled from a small JavaScript object. Close with the button, the backdrop or the Escape key. The hotspots pulse so readers know they are clickable. The annotated map, the labelled diagram, the "click the object to learn about it" are all this.

Swap the SVG for your own drawing or photo. Position each hotspot with left and top percentages. Add an entry to INFO for each one.
